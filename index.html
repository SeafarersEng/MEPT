What is MEPT?
MEPT ဆိုတာဘာလဲ ?

MEPT stands for the Maritime English Proficiency Test.It is a specialized English language examination designed specifically for seafarers and maritime professionals.

Why Practice?
ဘာကြောင့် လေ့ကျင့်ရတာလဲ ?

STCW requires effective communication. Our platform helps you master the language skills needed for safety and teamwork.
